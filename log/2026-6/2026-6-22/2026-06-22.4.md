


## 4.2 give_food_to_hungry_person 结果

必须产生：

```text id="0k9sjg"
Fact:
actor_gave_food_to_target

State Change:
target.hunger high -> medium
player.food_count -1 或 player.food_count 记录为变化

Relationship Change:
target -> player gratitude +1
target -> player trust +1
target -> player fear -1

Memory Seed:
target remembers received_help
```

如果当前 player food_count 只存在于 context，不适合直接写 StateStore，也必须在 TransactionResult.state_changes 中记录。
